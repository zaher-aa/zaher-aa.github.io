Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Midad is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels «مداد - قصص» and "Midad - Stories". It uses the YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, Midad uploads videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channels and reads those channels' statistics (views, watch time, likes, comments, subscribers).

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens are kept only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. Channel statistics are saved only on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Other people's data

Midad does not collect personal data from viewers or from anyone else.

Google and YouTube

By using Midad you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.
